Lunar phase changes approximately every 7 days.

The lunar phase is determined by the relative positions of the Sun, Moon, and Earth.>

There are eight primary lunar phases: New Moon, Waxing Crescent, First Quarter, Waxing Gibbous, Full Moon, Waning Gibbous, Last Quarter, and Waning Crescent.

The lunar cycle, also known as the synodic month, lasts approximately 29.5 days.
